UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM

Types of Pollution

Pollution affects our air, water, land and the world around us.

💨

Air Pollution

Harmful gases, smoke and tiny particles released into the atmosphere can affect people, animals and plants.

Learn about Air Pollution →
💧

Water Pollution

Waste, chemicals and other pollutants can contaminate rivers, lakes and oceans.

Learn about Water Pollution →
🌍

Land Pollution

Improper waste disposal and harmful chemicals can damage soil and natural ecosystems.

Learn about Land Pollution →
🔊

Noise Pollution

Excessive and unwanted sound from traffic, machines and other sources can disturb people and wildlife.

TAKE ACTION

Pollution can be controlled.

Every solution begins with a simple change in the way we live, travel, consume and dispose of things.

🌬️

CONTROL AIR POLLUTION

Use public transport, walk or cycle when possible. Avoid unnecessary burning and maintain vehicles properly.

YOUR ACTION → BREATHE CLEANER
💧

PROTECT WATER

Never throw plastic, chemicals or waste into rivers, lakes or drains. Use water responsibly.

YOUR ACTION → KEEP WATER CLEAN
🌱

PROTECT LAND

Separate waste, compost organic materials and avoid dumping garbage in open spaces.

YOUR ACTION → PROTECT THE SOIL
♻️

REDUCE PLASTIC

Carry reusable bags and bottles and choose reusable products instead of unnecessary single-use plastic.

YOUR ACTION → USE IT AGAIN
⚡

SAVE ENERGY

Switch off lights and devices when not needed. Choose efficient appliances and cleaner energy sources.

YOUR ACTION → USE ENERGY WISELY
🌍

INSPIRE OTHERS

Share environmental awareness with friends, family and your community. Small actions become stronger together.

YOUR ACTION → CREATE CHANGE
